# Material capture judgment

Capture the strongest checkable anchor: measurement, code path, versioned reproducible observation, before/after data, sourced resource, or concrete first-hand example.

Kinds:

- `finding`: investigation discovery;
- `experiment`: controlled method and result;
- `pitfall`: repeatable trap;
- `tool-comparison`: evidenced tradeoff;
- `opinion`: experience-backed view;
- `resource`: verified recommendation with a reason.

Verification:

- `verified`: measured, tested, or directly observed;
- `unverified`: sourced or plausible but not personally tested;
- `hypothesis`: inference awaiting evidence.

Maturity follows available evidence: `raw` preserves a fact, `incubating` has an angle and needs evidence, `ready` stands alone with verified claims, `published` has shipped, `archived` is retained for deduplication.

Link fragments from the same investigation or when one supports, extends, contradicts, or follows another. Capture setup/variables/results for experiments; symptom/investigation/cause/fix for debugging; current cause, scope, and measurements for PRs/issues.
