# Hacker News

Write as an engineer sharing with technically literate, skeptical peers.

For Show HN:

```text
Show HN: <name> – <plain description>

<problem or timely reason>
<implementation detail, number, or tradeoff>
<meaningful limitation>
<project link>
```

Keep the main post near 200 words when that preserves the evidence. Use English for the paste-ready post and add a Chinese summary for the user's review. Titles stay descriptive and preserve canonical casing.

For comments, answer the specific technical point, acknowledge real limitations, and explain the tradeoff. Offer comment strategy when requested.
